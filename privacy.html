Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Turf ("we") helps local businesses pick sponsorships using their own customer address data. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what we do with it. Plain version: we ask for as little as possible, we use it only to build your audit, and we never sell it.

What we collect

  • Your contact details. When you book an audit, our scheduling tool (Cal.com) collects your name and email so we can reach you.
  • Your customers' addresses. You send us a CSV with one customer address per row. We ask for addresses only — no customer names, emails, or phone numbers. Please don't send them.
  • Basic site data. This site itself sets no cookies and runs no analytics of its own. Our booking page is hosted by Cal.com and subject to their privacy policy.

How we use it

Your address data is used for one thing: building your Turf Audit — the customer heatmap, demographic overlay, and ranked sponsorship list. We do not use it for marketing, we do not combine it with other businesses' data, and we do not train models on it.

Who we share it with

No one, except the service providers needed to deliver your audit (website hosting, scheduling), who are bound to keep it confidential. We never sell your data or your customers' addresses, and we never share them with sponsors, leagues, or advertisers.

How long we keep it

We delete your customer address data after your audit is delivered, or sooner if you ask. Your own contact details are kept as a business record unless you ask us to delete them.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to show, correct, or delete the data we hold about you or your customers. Email hello@getturf.co and we'll handle it promptly.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the data you send us — encrypted transfer, limited access, deletion after delivery. No system is perfectly secure, so please don't send anything beyond the address list we ask for.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above. Continued use of the service after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about privacy: hello@getturf.co.
